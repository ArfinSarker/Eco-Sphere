Flowering Plants:
 Rose: 
Roses are classic flowering plants known for their fragrance, beauty, and variety, perfect for gardens and bouquets.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 30–50 cm
•	Blooms: Year-round in tropical climates
•	Selling Points: Fragrant flowers, decorative, ideal for gifting or garden.
Hibiscus: 
Hibiscus flowers bloom in vibrant colors, adding tropical charm and attracting pollinators.
•	Age: 1 year
•	Size: 40–60 cm tall
•	Blooms: Summer–Autumn
•	Selling Points: Large, colorful blooms, attracts pollinators, easy care.
Marigold: 
Marigolds are hardy flowers with bright hues, often used in gardens and festive decorations.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Blooms: Summer–Monsoon
•	Selling Points: Vibrant color, low maintenance, seasonal décor.
Jasmine: 
Jasmine is a fragrant flowering plant, ideal for garden borders and enhancing aromatic appeal.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 30–50 cm
•	Blooms: Year-round in tropical climates
•	Selling Points: Fragrant, decorative, ideal for balconies and gardens.
Sunflower: 
Sunflowers have large golden blooms that bring cheerfulness and attract pollinators.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 60–120 cm
•	Blooms: Summer
•	Selling Points: Bright blooms, easy to grow, attracts bees and butterflies.
Bougainvillea: 
Bougainvillea is a climbing plant with colorful bracts, perfect for fences and trellises.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 50–100 cm
•	Blooms: Spring–Summer
•	Selling Points: Vibrant bracts, climbing habit, low maintenance.
Chrysanthemum: 
Chrysanthemums bloom in diverse colors, enhancing gardens and seasonal floral displays.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Blooms: Autumn–Winter
•	Selling Points: Seasonal colors, decorative, long-lasting blooms.
Petunia: 
Petunias are easy-to-grow flowers with vibrant colors, ideal for pots and garden beds.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 20–30 cm
•	Blooms: Summer–Monsoon
•	Selling Points: Colorful, low maintenance, suitable for pots or garden beds.
Ixora: 
Ixora has clustered small flowers in bright colors, suitable for hedges and decoration.
•	Age: 1 year
•	Size: 30–50 cm
•	Blooms: Spring–Autumn
•	Selling Points: Dense flowers, vibrant colors, ideal for hedges.
Zinnia: 
Zinnias are resilient flowering plants that bloom abundantly, adding bright colors to gardens.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Blooms: Summer–Monsoon
•	Selling Points: Colorful blooms, hardy, attracts butterflies.
