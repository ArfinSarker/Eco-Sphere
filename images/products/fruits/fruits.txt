Fruit Plants:
Mango:
Mango trees provide delicious tropical fruit and shade, perfect for home gardens or commercial cultivation.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 60–120 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer
•	Selling Points: Sweet fruit, ornamental value, tropical appeal.
Guava:
Guava trees bear nutritious fruits, ideal for garden or backyard cultivation.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 50–100 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer–Monsoon
•	Selling Points: Edible fruits, easy care, fast-growing.
Lemon:
Lemon trees yield fresh citrus fruit and fragrant foliage.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 40–80 cm
•	Fruiting: Year-round in tropical climates
•	Selling Points: Edible fruit, ornamental greenery, low maintenance.
Banana:
Banana plants produce sweet fruits and have large tropical leaves.
•	Age: 1 year
•	Size: 1–2 m
•	Fruiting: Year-round in tropical regions
•	Selling Points: Fruit-bearing, decorative foliage, fast-growing.
Papaya:
Papaya trees are fast-growing with sweet, nutritious fruits.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 1–1.5 m
•	Fruiting: Year-round
•	Selling Points: Nutritious fruits, tropical garden addition, low maintenance.
Pomegranate:
Pomegranate plants produce vibrant red flowers and edible fruits.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 50–80 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer
•	Selling Points: Decorative and edible, drought-tolerant, easy to grow.
Litchi:
Litchi trees provide sweet, seasonal fruits with ornamental foliage.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 80–120 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer
•	Selling Points: Seasonal fruit, decorative foliage, high yield potential.
Jujube:
Jujube is a hardy fruit tree with edible seasonal fruits.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 60–100 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer–Autumn
•	Selling Points: Low maintenance, edible fruit, drought-tolerant.
Star Fruit:
Star fruit trees produce uniquely shaped edible fruits.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 80–120 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer
•	Selling Points: Decorative and edible, tropical garden addition.
Olive:
Olive trees provide small fruits and evergreen foliage.
•	Age: 2–3 years
•	Size: 80–120 cm
•	Fruiting: Summer–Autumn
•	Selling Points: Mediterranean appeal, edible fruits, decorative foliage.
