Indoor Plants: 
Snake Plant:
Snake plants are hardy indoor plants known for purifying air and thriving in low-light areas, adding greenery to homes and offices.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 20–40 cm tall
•	Blooms: Rarely, in late summer
•	Selling Points: Low maintenance, excellent air purifier, ideal for home or office.
Money Plant: 
Money plants symbolize prosperity and are easy-to-grow indoor vines that improve aesthetics and indoor air quality.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 30–50 cm vine
•	Blooms: Rare
•	Selling Points: Easy to grow in soil or water, auspicious, fast-growing.
Peace Lily: 
Peace lilies are elegant indoor plants with white flowers, enhancing indoor beauty and purifying air efficiently.
•	Age: 1 year
•	Size: 25–40 cm tall
•	Blooms: Summer, white flowers
•	Selling Points: Low-light tolerant, air-purifying, visually appealing.
ZZ Plant: 
ZZ plants are drought-tolerant indoor plants with glossy leaves, ideal for offices and low-light corners.
•	Age: 8–12 months
•	Size: 30–45 cm
•	Blooms: Rare
•	Selling Points: Low maintenance, survives low light, decorative foliage.
Spider Plant: 
Spider plants are resilient indoor greenery with arching leaves, improving indoor air and easy to propagate.
•	Age: 6–9 months
•	Size: 25–35 cm
•	Blooms: Small white flowers, spring-summer
•	Selling Points: Air-purifying, easy to propagate, adaptable.
Rubber Plant: 
Rubber plants are large-leaved indoor species that thrive in moderate light, adding elegance and purifying indoor air.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 50–80 cm
•	Blooms: Rare indoors
•	Selling Points: Large foliage, indoor air purification, ornamental value.
Aglaonema: 
Aglaonema is a low-maintenance indoor plant with patterned leaves, providing aesthetic greenery in shaded interiors.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 25–50 cm
•	Blooms: Rare indoors
•	Selling Points: Easy care, decorative leaves, indoor-friendly.
Dracaena: 
Dracaena is a tall indoor plant with striking foliage, improving air quality and visual appeal.
•	Age: 1 year
•	Size: 40–70 cm
•	Blooms: Rare
•	Selling Points: Indoor décor, air purification, visually appealing foliage.
Croton: 
Croton is a vibrant indoor plant with colorful leaves, perfect for decorative indoor pots.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 30–60 cm
•	Blooms: Rare
•	Selling Points: Colorful foliage, indoor decoration, low maintenance.
Calathea: 
Calathea is an ornamental indoor plant with patterned leaves, adding unique beauty to home interiors.
•	Age: 6–9 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Blooms: Rare
•	Selling Points: Decorative leaves, shade-loving, indoor air friendly.
