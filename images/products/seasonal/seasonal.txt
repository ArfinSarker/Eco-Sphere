Seasonal Plants:
Marigold:
Marigolds are hardy seasonal flowers that add vibrant orange and yellow colors to gardens.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 20–40 cm
•	Blooms: Summer–Monsoon
•	Selling Points: Bright colors, low maintenance, festive decoration.
Chrysanthemum:
Chrysanthemums bloom in diverse colors, ideal for autumn gardens.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Blooms: Autumn–Winter
•	Selling Points: Seasonal decorative blooms, easy care, long-lasting flowers.
