Succulents:
 Aloe Vera: 
Aloe Vera succulents store water, requiring minimal care and offering medicinal benefits.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Harvest: Leaves usable year-round
•	Selling Points: Decorative and medicinal, low water needs.
Jade Plant: 
Jade plants are hardy succulents symbolizing prosperity and easy to maintain.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 20–40 cm
•	Blooms: Rare indoors
•	Selling Points: Decorative, low maintenance, symbolic prosperity.
Echeveria: 
Echeveria has rosette-shaped leaves, ideal for pots and decorative arrangements.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 10–20 cm
•	Blooms: Small seasonal flowers
•	Selling Points: Aesthetic arrangement, low water needs, indoor décor.
Haworthia: 
Haworthia are small succulents suitable for indoor pots, requiring minimal sunlight.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 5–15 cm
•	Blooms: Small flowers occasionally
•	Selling Points: Miniature indoor décor, low maintenance.
Moon Cactus: 
Moon Cactus is a colorful grafted succulent, perfect for decorative indoor display.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 5–15 cm
•	Blooms: Rare
•	Selling Points: Vibrant, decorative, easy care.
Barrel Cactus: 
Barrel cactus is a round succulent that adds desert-like charm to gardens or pots.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 15–30 cm
•	Blooms: Seasonal small flowers
•	Selling Points: Hardy, decorative, minimal care.
Opuntia: 
Opuntia, or prickly pear, is a cactus species with edible pads and decorative value.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 30–50 cm
•	Blooms: Spring–Summer
•	Selling Points: Edible pads, decorative cactus, low water needs.
Kalanchoe: 
Kalanchoe is a flowering succulent that blooms indoors, requiring minimal care.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 15–25 cm
•	Blooms: Winter–Spring
•	Selling Points: Colorful flowers, indoor décor, low maintenance.
Sedum: 
Sedum is a hardy succulent suitable for rock gardens and container planting.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 10–20 cm
•	Blooms: Summer
•	Selling Points: Easy care, drought-tolerant, decorative ground cover.
String of Pearls: 
String of Pearls is a trailing succulent with bead-like leaves, ideal for hanging pots.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 20–40 cm trailing
•	Blooms: Small flowers occasionally
•	Selling Points: Hanging décor, low maintenance, indoor/outdoor adaptability.
