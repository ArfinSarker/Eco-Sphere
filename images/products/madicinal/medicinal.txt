Medicinal Plants: 
Tulsi: 
Tulsi is a sacred herb with medicinal properties, widely grown for health and spiritual purposes.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 20–30 cm
•	Harvest: Year-round
•	Selling Points: Purifying, immunity-boosting, easy to grow in pots.
Neem: 
Neem is a medicinal tree known for its antibacterial and antifungal benefits.
•	Age: 1–2 years
•	Size: 50–100 cm
•	Harvest: Leaves usable year-round
•	Selling Points: Natural medicine, low maintenance, ideal for garden.
Aloe Vera: 
Aloe Vera is a succulent with soothing gel, widely used for skin and health remedies.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 25–40 cm
•	Harvest: Leaves usable year-round
•	Selling Points: Medicinal gel, low maintenance, indoor or outdoor planting.
Lemongrass: 
Lemongrass is a fragrant herb used in cooking, teas, and traditional medicine.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 30–60 cm
•	Harvest: Year-round
•	Selling Points: Culinary and medicinal use, aromatic, easy to maintain.
Mint: 
Mint is a refreshing herb, easy to grow, and used in drinks, food, and remedies.
•	Age: 2–4 months
•	Size: 20–30 cm
•	Harvest: Multiple times a year
•	Selling Points: Fresh leaves for cooking, easy propagation, aromatic.
Curry Leaf: 
Curry leaf plants provide aromatic leaves used in cooking and aid digestion.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 30–50 cm
•	Harvest: Year-round
•	Selling Points: Culinary herb, medicinal, low maintenance.
Turmeric: 
Turmeric is a medicinal rhizome used in cooking, with anti-inflammatory and health benefits.
•	Age: 6–9 months
•	Size: 20–30 cm (rhizome)
•	Harvest: Monsoon–Autumn
•	Selling Points: Culinary and medicinal use, easy to grow.
Ginger: 
Ginger is a medicinal root used for flavor and natural remedies.
•	Age: 6–9 months
•	Size: 15–25 cm (rhizome)
•	Harvest: Monsoon–Autumn
•	Selling Points: Culinary and medicinal use, easy cultivation.
Thankuni: 
Thankuni is an herb with medicinal properties for memory and skin health.
•	Age: 3–6 months
•	Size: 15–25 cm
•	Harvest: Year-round
•	Selling Points: Health benefits, easy to grow, edible leaves.
Ashwagandha: 
Ashwagandha is a medicinal plant known for stress relief and overall wellness.
•	Age: 6–12 months
•	Size: 30–50 cm
•	Harvest: Year-round roots usable
•	Selling Points: Medicinal root, low maintenance, ideal for herb gardens.
